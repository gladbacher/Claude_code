# Accuracy Ledger — The Rumour, Graded

Public record of every rumour graded on the show. Updated every week by `/transfer-week` (Step 3) **before** new rumours are graded: search for outcomes on all OPEN entries, update them, recompute the hit rate.

**Scoring convention:** a grade counts as a HIT when a rumour graded ≥7 is CONFIRMED, or a rumour graded ≤4 DIES or is RECYCLED. Grades 5–6 are excluded from the hit rate (they were calls of genuine uncertainty). Hit rate = hits / resolved gradeable entries.
