# Direction Memo — July 2026: The Window Is Open

Dated update to the series direction based on Gary Stevenson's recent output and the current political moment. Supersedes nothing structurally — the spine, tone rules, and pipeline stand — but re-weights the next 4–6 weeks.

## What changed

1. **Channel 4 documentary** — *How to Get Filthy Rich* (Stevenson, 90 min) airs **Wed 8 July, 9pm**, on YouTube from **12 July**. The largest mainstream attention spike the wealth-tax argument has ever had.
2. **The political window** — Stevenson's June framing: closest to wealth taxes in 40 years; the incoming Burnham government faces a binary choice between taxing wealth and continuing to dismantle the welfare state.
3. **The counter-attack has started** — economic journalists and publications are now arguing *against* wealth taxes at volume (his 14 June video catalogues it). The debate has moved from ignored to contested.

## Direction changes

### 1. Documentary week is a special (8–12 July)
- **Reactive short, 8–9 July**: "Watch the documentary on Wednesday. Then come back here, because there's a part of this story it won't have time to tell you — the part about *your* pension and *your* house." Position as companion, never competitor.
- **Master video, w/c 12 July** (timed to the YouTube release): "What *How to Get Filthy Rich* didn't have time to tell you" — take his macro case as read, deliver stage two (the middle-class asset extraction mechanisms). Search terms around the doc title will spike; caption and title accordingly.
- The differentiation rule: Gary explains *why the rich get richer*; this series shows *where it's coming out of your accounts*. Every doc-week piece must land on a viewer-specific asset (pension, house, savings, inheritance).

### 2. Re-weight the lens rotation
While the media counter-offensive runs, REBUTTAL weeks may run consecutively (suspending the "different lens than last week" rule). Each anti-wealth-tax piece by a major outlet is a pre-promoted episode: steelman it honestly, dismantle it, end with the dinner-table one-liner. Track which rebuttals recur — the recurring ones become evergreen shorts.

### 3. Register shift: from "see the machine" to "the window is open"
Script 5's closing argument — this gets fixed while the middle class still has power, or not at all — is now current affairs, not rhetoric. CTAs can escalate accordingly: MP letters now reference a live policy debate, not a hypothetical. Urgency must stay *evidenced* (named votes, named consultations, named dates), never performative — the tone rules still bind.

### 4. Vocabulary to adopt (with attribution)
- **"Inheritocracy"** (vs meritocracy) — one-word frame for the inheritance-interception argument already in the series.
- **The "binary choice"** — wealth tax or continued dismantling of services. Sharpens "What actually needs to happen" sections.
- Bond-market/"who lends to the government" literacy — Stevenson's explainer signals the next rebuttal frontier ("the markets won't allow it"); prepare a REBUTTAL episode on it.

## Review date
Re-assess after the documentary's YouTube run (late July): if the wealth-tax debate stays hot, keep the re-weighting; if it cools, revert to the standard four-lens rotation.
