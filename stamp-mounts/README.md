# Stamp Mount Template Builder

A simple program for stamp collectors. It lets you design a page of stamp
mount outlines, write a description under each one, and print it — ready
to mount your stamps on.

No internet connection is needed and nothing has to be installed.

## How to open it

1. Open the folder called `stamp-mounts`.
2. Double-click the file called **`index.html`**.
3. It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari) —
   the same program you use to look at websites.

**Tip for family members setting this up:** create a desktop shortcut to
`index.html` and rename it "Stamp Pages" so it is one double-click away.

## How to use it

Everything is explained inside the program — click
**"How do I use this? Click here"** at the top of the left-hand panel.

In short:

1. Work down the left-hand panel from top to bottom. The page on the
   right shows exactly what will be printed.
2. Choose your paper size, then the size of your mounts and how many
   you want on the page.
3. Click the dotted line under any mount and type a description,
   for example *"Penny Black 1840"*.
4. Click the big blue **Print my page** button. In the window that
   opens, click **Print** — or choose **Save as PDF** to keep a copy.

Your work is saved automatically on your computer, so you can close the
program and carry on later.

## If something goes wrong

- **The message at the bottom says "Too big for the page"** — the mounts
  don't all fit. Make them smaller, or reduce "How many across" /
  "How many down".
- **You want to wipe everything and start fresh** — click
  **Start again from scratch**. It will ask you to confirm first.
- **The printout is the wrong size** — in the print window, make sure
  the paper size matches what you chose in the program, and that any
  "Scale" or "Fit to page" option is set to **100%**.
