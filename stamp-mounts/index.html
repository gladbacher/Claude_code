📭 Stamp Mount Builder
How do I use this? Click here
  1. Work down this panel from top to bottom. The page on the right shows exactly what will be printed — it updates as you go.
  2. Choose your paper, then how big your mounts should be and how many you want.
  3. To describe a stamp, click the dotted line underneath any mount on the page and type — for example “Penny Black 1840”.
  4. When you are happy, click the big blue Print button at the bottom. In the window that opens, click Print — or choose Save as PDF to keep a copy.

Your work is saved automatically on this computer, so it will still be here if you close and come back later.
